## 1. Executive Incubation Summary

As part of the Entrepreneurship Vertical in Design Experience III, **AgroLoop** was developed not merely as a simulated academic exercise, but as an investable, commercially viable cleantech venture. 

To bridge the gap between academic prototyping and market launch, active interactions, formal applications, and presentation defenses were conducted with institutional Technology Business Incubators (TBIs), University Innovation Councils, and startup pitch competitions during the academic semester (July–October 2026).

### Key Highlights & Institutional Milestones:
1. **INNOVATE 360° (2026–27) Technical Pitch Competition**:

   - **Date of Presentation**: 4th September 2026.
   - **Outcome**: **Secured 3rd Position** across competitive project entries.
   - **Certification**: Officially awarded to Veer Nagda by Dr. Priti Rumao (HoD, Computer Engineering) and Professor in-Charge.
2. **Atal Incubation Centre (AIC - NMIMS) Pre-Incubation Pipeline**:
   - Formal application and pitch deck submitted for Cohort 8 Pre-Incubation Programme.
   - Mentoring session on Unit Economics, Municipal Regulatory Clearances, and Biochar Carbon Credit Monetization.
3. **Agri-Business Incubator (R-ABI) / KVK Consultation**:
   - Engagement with Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Palghar and R-ABI (Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana - RAFTAAR) mentors for farmer validation trials and FCO (Fertilizer Control Order, 1985) testing protocols.

### Milestone Accomplishments:
- **Phase A (Discovery & Feasibility)**: Validated willingness-to-pay across 45 commercial kitchens in Bandra/Andheri; quantified average daily wet waste generation (145 kg/restaurant).
- **Phase B (Prototype Engineering)**: Built full-stack responsive web application with 60 generated pages, offline local storage fallback, dynamic impact calculation engine, and AI crop advisory desk.

- **Phase D (Pilot Deployment & Incubation Filing)**: Finalized 5,000 sq ft pilot bio-refinery hub blueprint in Mahape, Navi Mumbai; conducted initial biochar soil trials showing 28% increase in moisture retention.
